Support

Sprint Ledger for Jira Cloud · updated 23 September 2026

Write to support@dyordanov.com. Every message is read and answered by the developer of the app, in English.

How fast you get an answer

Your messageFirst answer
The app has stopped working on your site: the reconstruction fails, the fields or the functions stopped answering, the admin page does not load. Put Urgent in the subject line.Within 24 hours, weekends included
Everything else: a number you want explained, a question about setup, a feature requestWithin one business day (Monday to Friday)
Private beta sites, until the app is listed on the Atlassian MarketplaceThe same day

The first answer says what is known and what happens next. A fix that needs an app update follows as soon as it is built and checked; updates install on your site by themselves.

What to include

  • Your site's address (yoursite.atlassian.net) and the version shown at the top of the app's admin page.
  • What you did, what you expected and what you saw, with the time it happened and your time zone.
  • For a number that looks wrong: the sprint's name or ID, the issue keys, and what Sprint Ledger and Jira's Sprint Report each say. How the numbers compare with the Sprint Report lists the differences that are expected.
  • A screenshot, if it helps.

Never send passwords or API tokens: Sprint Ledger never needs them, and nobody supporting it will ask for them.

Before you write

Most questions are already answered on this site: the FAQ, two things to check first (a board whose filter is not shared, fields that are not on a team-managed project's layout), and the administration guide. The changelog lists every update.

Feature requests and bug reports in public

You can also open an issue at github.com/damyor/sprint-ledger-docs/issues, where other customers can see and add to it. That repository is public, so leave out anything about your site you would not publish: issue keys, sprint names, people. Email is always fine instead.

Security and privacy

A security vulnerability goes to the address above with Security in the subject line, not to a public issue; the security page says what happens next. Requests about personal data are covered by the privacy policy.
