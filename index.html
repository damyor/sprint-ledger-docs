Sprint Ledger for Jira

Sprint scope change and carry-over as JQL and columns, for every sprint you have ever run.

Ask Jira what was added after you committed, what was removed, what was completed and what keeps rolling over: issue in addedAfterSprintStart("Sprint 42"). Sprint Ledger rebuilds each sprint from the history Jira already keeps, so it answers for sprints that closed years ago as well as for today's, and it runs entirely on Atlassian's infrastructure.

The app is in a private beta for Jira Cloud. Sign up for the beta; you get one email, with the install link.
