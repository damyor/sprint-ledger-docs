Sprint Ledger for Jira

Sprint scope change and carryover as JQL and columns, for every sprint you have ever run.

Ask Jira what was added after you committed, what was removed, what was completed and what keeps rolling over: issue in addedAfterSprintStart("Sprint 42").

The app is in development. Documentation, the JQL function reference, the privacy policy and the support page will be published here before the Marketplace listing.

Sprint Ledger is an independent app and is not affiliated with Atlassian.
